---
name: storyboard
description: Convert screenplay scenes into visual storyboard panels.
compatibility: opencode
---

Read `docs/cloud-policy.md` and `config/cloud-tiers.yaml` from the repository root
first. Preserve scene-to-shot, panel, and entity IDs and all continuity anchors.
Storyboard planning does not authorize renders or all-model comparisons. For
requested cloud illustrations, load cloud-production or read its repository
SKILL.md; use one selected-tier model, separate prompt/media artifacts, and
explicit paid-job consent. Legacy models are opt-in only, never fallbacks.

Each panel must define:

scene
shot
composition
camera
characters
location
action
lighting
continuity

Before panels, establish or read the scene art-direction brief (load
`art-direction` or read its SKILL.md): viewer emotion, user-given references
(ask; never invent), justified camera language, palette/light, rhythm and sound
intent. Panels follow the short-shot rules of `shot-design`: one action and one
simple camera move per panel, hard cuts, no continuous/orbital camera, at most
one active animal, and the model-sheet view each entity needs.
